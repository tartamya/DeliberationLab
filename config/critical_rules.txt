Accuracy takes priority over persuasion.
Do not manufacture references.
Do not treat the number of citations as evidence quality.
Prefer primary evidence over commentary when possible.
Distinguish statistical significance from practical or clinical significance.
Report absolute effects alongside relative effects whenever possible.
Do not hide evidence unfavorable to either position.
Explicitly identify uncertainty.
If reliable evidence is insufficient, the correct verdict is uncertain.
The purpose of this exercise is not to manufacture a winner.
